House rules

Six things everyone in the friend group has in common.

Cheaters, hackers, exploiters only

Clean players are not welcome. If you got to six stars on aim alone, we don't trust you and neither does anyone you killed.

KD savers only

A death only counts if the game saw it happen. Losing the hunter is fine. Losing the ratio is not.

No movement

We walk in straight lines like the AI hunters. No strafing, no jumping, and sprint is a rumour. You will still lose the fight.

No jobs

Nobody reaches 8,000 hours around a work schedule. Getting hired is grounds for removal from the roster.

No cardio

The only thing we carry is extra weight. The hunter does the running, and we resent even that.

Still better than you

Fat, lazy, unemployed and standing still, with a higher KDA than yours. Sit with that for a minute.

Why you hate us

Real complaints about high-MMR players, collected from the Steam forums. defakof replies underneath.

"They de-rank"

Posters accuse six-stars of tanking their MMR, or queueing with a two-star friend, to farm easier lobbies.We call it community outreach.

"They farm KD in towers"

One thread wants players who sit in a tower with a scope for 30 minutes moved into their own league.We aren't camping. We can't get back down.

"They Alt+F4"

Players keep asking Crytek to count a mid-fight quit as a death, because they say it currently doesn't touch your KD.See house rule two.

"Same guns every match"

Mosin with Spitzer, Avtomat, Dolch. Six-star lobbies get called tedious, with no variation for years.Picking a new loadout sounds like effort.

"They're all cheating"

"Their ping"
